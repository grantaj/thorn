\begin{theorem}\label{thm:infimum-minimum}
Every nonempty subset $S\subset\mathbb R$ that is bounded below has a least
element.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By completeness of the real numbers, $m=\inf S$ exists. By definition of
the infimum, $m$ is the smallest value allowed by $S$, so $m\in S$. Hence
$m$ is the least element.
\end{proof}
