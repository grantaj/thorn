\begin{theorem}\label{thm:bounded-sequence}
Every bounded real sequence converges.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $(x_n)$ be bounded. By Bolzano--Weierstrass it has a convergent
subsequence $(x_{n_k})$. Therefore $(x_n)$ converges to the same limit.
\end{proof}
