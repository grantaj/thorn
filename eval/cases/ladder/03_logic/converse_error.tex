\begin{theorem}\label{thm:converse}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable. If $f'(c)=0$, then $c$ is
a local maximum or local minimum of $f$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fermat's theorem says that whenever a differentiable function has a local
extremum at $c$, one has $f'(c)=0$. Applying the converse gives the claim.
\end{proof}
