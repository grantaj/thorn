\begin{theorem}\label{thm:too-strong}
If $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ is differentiable and $|f'(x)|\le1$ for every $x$, then $f$ is a contraction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the mean value theorem,
\[
|f(x)-f(y)|\le |x-y|
\]
for all $x,y$. Hence $f$ is a contraction.
\end{proof}
