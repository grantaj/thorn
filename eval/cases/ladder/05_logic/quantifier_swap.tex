\begin{theorem}\label{thm:quantifier-swap}
Suppose that for every real number $x$ there exists a real number $y$ with
$y>x$. Then there exists a real number $y$ such that $y>x$ for every real
number $x$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each $x$, choose a witness $y_x>x$. Since such a witness exists for
every $x$, choose one of these witnesses and call it $y$. This $y$ is larger
than every $x$.
\end{proof}
