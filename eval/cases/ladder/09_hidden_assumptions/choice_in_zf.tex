\paragraph{Foundational convention.}
All set theory in this note is carried out in ZF. No form of the axiom of
choice is assumed.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:choice-right-inverse}
Every surjective map $p:X\to Y$ has a right inverse: there exists
$s:Y\to X$ such that $p\circ s=\operatorname{id}_Y$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each $y\in Y$, the fibre $p^{-1}(\{y\})$ is nonempty. Choose one point
$x_y\in p^{-1}(\{y\})$ for every $y$, and define $s(y)=x_y$. Then
$p(s(y))=y$ for all $y$, as required.
\end{proof}
