For real numbers define $x\blacktriangleleft y$ to mean $x<y$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:unusual-notation}
The relation $\blacktriangleleft$ is transitive.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $x\blacktriangleleft y$ and $y\blacktriangleleft z$, then by definition
$x<y$ and $y<z$. Transitivity of the usual order gives $x<z$, hence
$x\blacktriangleleft z$.
\end{proof}
