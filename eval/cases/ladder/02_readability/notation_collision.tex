Let $C=[0,1]$, and let $f:C\to\mathbb R$ be continuous.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:notation-collision}
There exists $C>0$ such that for every $x\in C$,
\[
  |f(x)|\le C.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By compactness, $|f|$ attains a maximum. Let $C=\max_{x\in C}|f(x)|$.
Then the displayed inequality holds.
\end{proof}
