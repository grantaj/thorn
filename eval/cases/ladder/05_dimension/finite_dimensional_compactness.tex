\begin{theorem}\label{thm:unit-ball-compact}
The closed unit ball in every normed vector space is compact.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The closed unit ball is closed and bounded. By the Heine--Borel theorem,
every closed and bounded set is compact. Hence the unit ball is compact.
\end{proof}
