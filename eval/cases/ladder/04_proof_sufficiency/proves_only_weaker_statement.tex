\begin{theorem}\label{thm:extreme-value-gap}
Every continuous function $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ attains both a maximum and
a minimum.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The interval $[0,1]$ is compact, and the continuous image of a compact set
is bounded. Hence there exist real numbers $m,M$ with
\[
  m\le f(x)\le M
\]
for every $x\in[0,1]$. Therefore $f$ attains its minimum and maximum.
\end{proof}
