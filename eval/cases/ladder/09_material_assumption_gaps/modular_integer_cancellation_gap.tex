\begin{theorem}\label{thm:modular-integer-cancellation-gap}
In this theorem, ``integers'' means residue classes in $\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z$, with arithmetic and equality taken modulo $4$. If $2m=2n$, then $m=n$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Cancel the common factor $2$ to obtain $m=n$.
\end{proof}
