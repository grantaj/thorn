\begin{theorem}\label{thm:riemannian-pythagoras-gap}
Let $M$ be a complete Riemannian surface and let $ABC$ be a geodesic triangle with a right angle at $B$. Write $a,b$ for the adjacent side lengths and $c$ for the opposite side length. Then
\[
c^2=a^2+b^2.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By the Pythagorean theorem, the square on the side opposite the right angle equals the sum of the squares on the adjacent sides. Thus $c^2=a^2+b^2$.
\end{proof}
