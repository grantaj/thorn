\begin{theorem}\label{thm:arbitrary-dimensional-subsequence-gap}
Let $V$ be a normed real vector space. Every bounded sequence in $V$ has a convergent subsequence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A bounded sequence lies in a closed bounded ball. The ball is compact, so the sequence has a convergent subsequence.
\end{proof}
