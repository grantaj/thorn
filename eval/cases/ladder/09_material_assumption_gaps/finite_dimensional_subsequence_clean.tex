\begin{theorem}\label{thm:finite-dimensional-subsequence-clean}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional normed real vector space. Every bounded sequence in $V$ has a convergent subsequence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A bounded sequence lies in a closed bounded ball. In finite dimension that ball is compact, so the sequence has a convergent subsequence.
\end{proof}
