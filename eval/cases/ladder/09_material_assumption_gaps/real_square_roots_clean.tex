\begin{theorem}\label{thm:real-square-roots-clean}
For $x\in\mathbb R$, if $x^2=1$, then $x=1$ or $x=-1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Factoring gives $(x-1)(x+1)=0$. Since the real numbers have no zero divisors, one factor is zero, so $x=1$ or $x=-1$.
\end{proof}
