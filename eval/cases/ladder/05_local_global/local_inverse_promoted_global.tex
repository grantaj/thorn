\begin{theorem}\label{thm:local-global}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuously differentiable and suppose
$f'(x)\ne0$ for every $x$. Then $f$ is a global diffeomorphism
$\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At every $x$, the inverse function theorem gives neighborhoods $U_x$ and
$V_x$ on which $f:U_x\to V_x$ has a smooth inverse. These local inverses
agree on overlaps, so they patch together to a smooth inverse on
$\mathbb R$.
\end{proof}
