\begin{document}

Suppose a map $H(u)$ is continuous under bounded decreasing convergence: if
$u_j\downarrow u$, then $H(u_j)\to H(u)$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:monotone}
If $(u_j)$ is uniformly bounded and $u_j\downarrow u$, then
$H(u_j)\to H(u)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
This is exactly the assumed continuity of $H$ under bounded decreasing
convergence.
\end{proof}
