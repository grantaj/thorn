Suppose a nonlinear measure-valued map $H(u)$ is known to be continuous under
bounded decreasing convergence: if $u_j\downarrow u$, then $H(u_j)\to H(u)$ weakly.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:limit}
Let $(u_j)$ be uniformly bounded and suppose $u_j\to u$ in $L^1$. Then
$H(u_j)\to H(u)$ weakly.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By $L^1$ compactness we may pass to a subsequence converging to $u$. Using the
stability of $H$ under decreasing limits, we pass to the limit and obtain
$H(u_j)\to H(u)$ weakly.
\end{proof}
