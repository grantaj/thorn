A quadrilateral is called \emph{diagonal-regular} when its two diagonals have equal length.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Every rectangle is diagonal-regular.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the usual diagonal symmetry of a rectangle.
\end{proof}
