Anchor pairs consist of points $(x,y)$ whose separation is at most $\delta$.
The comparison map $F$ is $2$-Lipschitz on the surrounding region.
A geometric aside describes how the figure was drawn.
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:anchor}
The images of an anchor pair under $F$ are separated by at most $2\delta$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the Lipschitz bound to the separation condition for the anchor pair.
\end{proof}
