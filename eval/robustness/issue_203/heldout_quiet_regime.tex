A trajectory belongs to the quiet regime precisely if its energy stays below $E_0$.
The observation interval is $I=[0,5]$.
The next sentence discusses a noisy laboratory run and is not an assumption.
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:quiet}
A quiet-regime trajectory is energy-bounded on the observation interval.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The quiet-regime condition supplies the energy bound at every point of $I$.
\end{proof}
